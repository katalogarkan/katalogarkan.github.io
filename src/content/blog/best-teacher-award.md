In 2025, I was honored to receive the **Best Teacher Award** at Satu Benih Boarding School. 

To some, this might look like a distraction from my tech career. Why would a Data Engineer care about teaching high school students? 

The truth is: **Teaching made me a better engineer.**

![Best Teacher Award Ceremony](/images/achievements/best-teacher-photo.jpg)
*Figure 1: Receiving the Best Teacher Award at Satu Benih Boarding School, 2025.*

## The Biggest Problem in Tech (It's Not the Code)

Many engineers are incredibly smart. They can write complex algorithms, build machine learning models, and optimize databases. 

But when they have to explain their work to a business stakeholder, they fail. They use jargon. They get frustrated when the client doesn't understand. 

In the real world, **communication is just as important as coding.**

## Translating "Tech" to "Business"

Teaching forced me to abandon technical jargon. When I was standing in front of a classroom of students with zero background in technology, I couldn't talk about "asynchronous programming" or "relational databases." 

I had to use analogies. I had to break down complex systems into simple, understandable concepts.

I learned to:
- **Listen first:** Understand what the audience already knows and what they need to achieve.
- **Use simple analogies:** A database isn't a "normalized set of tables"; it's a "digital filing cabinet."
- **Be patient and empathetic:** Nobody likes to feel stupid. A good communicator makes the listener feel smart.

## Why This Makes Me a Better Freelancer

When I work with a client on a data engineering project, I don't just hand over the code and disappear. 

I explain the architecture. I explain the cost implications of the cloud services we are using. I show them how to use their new dashboard so they can actually get value from it.

My clients don't need to be tech experts. They need someone who can **communicate clearly and build with empathy**. 

That is the skill I bring to every project, and it started with that Best Teacher Award.
